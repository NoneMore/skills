# AGENTS.md

## Purpose

This is a long-running research workspace. Preserve a traceable path from source material to notes, synthesis, and final outputs.

## Sources of truth

- `sources/` contains captured source material. Treat it as immutable evidence.
- `notes/` contains working interpretation and may change as understanding develops.
- `synthesis/` contains derived conclusions and may be revised or regenerated.
- `outputs/` contains audience-facing deliverables; do not treat publication prose as stronger evidence than its underlying sources.

When sources conflict, preserve the conflict in the synthesis instead of silently choosing the cleaner narrative.

## Research behavior

Separate source claims, observed evidence, and our inference. Label unsupported but useful ideas as hypotheses.

For material claims, numbers, dates, and direct attributions, preserve a source path or citation sufficient to find the evidence again.

When reviewing literature, prefer primary material when practical. Use secondary material for discovery and context, not as a silent substitute for available primary evidence.

## Changes and lifecycle

Preserve historical source material. Supersede stale synthesis by updating it or moving the old version to the workspace archive when an archive convention exists.

Before creating a new topic note, check whether an existing note is already the canonical home.

## Completion

A research task is complete when:
- material claims are traceable;
- meaningful counter-evidence is represented;
- unresolved uncertainty is explicit; and
- the requested output can be reached from the workspace's normal index or project structure.
